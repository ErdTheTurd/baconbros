What We Do

Full-cycle land services for the Horizon market.

From raw acreage to managed holdings, we handle the details that matter in desert country — water rights, access, zoning, and long-term land value.

Land Acquisition

We identify and evaluate parcels across the Horizon corridor — assessing title, access routes, utility proximity, and development potential before you commit capital.

Property Management

Ongoing oversight for holdings large and small: boundary maintenance, lease administration, vendor coordination, and compliance with local El Paso County requirements.

Land Sales

When it is time to divest, we market properties to qualified buyers, manage showings and negotiations, and guide transactions through closing with clear documentation.

Our Region

Horizon, Texas and the greater El Paso corridor.

Horizon sits at the edge of the Chihuahuan Desert, where open land, mountain views, and steady growth create real opportunity — and real complexity. Bacon Bros has operated in this market long enough to know which parcels hold value and which questions to ask first.

Whether you are holding ranch land, a residential lot, or commercial acreage along the I-10 corridor, we bring a practical, boots-on-the-ground perspective to every engagement.
