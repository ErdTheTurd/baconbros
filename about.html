About Bacon Bros

Built on local knowledge. Run with professional discipline.

Bacon Bros LLC was founded to give landowners in the Horizon area a partner who understands desert property — not just on paper, but on the ground.

Who We Are

A land company for people who take property seriously.

West Texas land is not generic. Water access, soil composition, flood zones, easement history, and county permitting all shape what a parcel is actually worth and what it can become. Bacon Bros was established to navigate that complexity on behalf of owners, buyers, and investors in the Horizon market.

We buy land when the fundamentals are right. We manage holdings with regular oversight and clear reporting. And when our clients are ready to sell, we position properties honestly and close deals without unnecessary friction.

Our work sits at the intersection of real estate and stewardship. Desert land requires patience, respect for the terrain, and relationships with the people who live and work nearby. That is the standard we hold ourselves to on every acre.

How we operate

  • Thorough title and access review before any acquisition
  • Transparent communication with property owners at every stage
  • Conservative underwriting — we would rather pass than overpay
  • Active management, not absentee ownership
  • Long-term relationships over one-time transactions
  • Respect for the land, the community, and local regulation

Our Focus Areas

What we bring to every engagement.

Due Diligence

Survey records, title chains, water rights, mineral reservations, and zoning — we review the details that determine whether a deal makes sense.
