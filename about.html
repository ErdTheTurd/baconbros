About Bacon Bros

We own the land. We sell it directly.

Bacon Bros LLC is not a brokerage or a land-management firm. We hold two specific parcels in the Horizon area and are offering them to qualified buyers — owner to buyer, with full transparency on what each piece of ground actually is.

Who We Are

A focused offering — not a catalog of listings.

Most land websites try to sell everything to everyone. Bacon Bros does the opposite. We own exactly two parcels in the Horizon / El Paso County market, and this site exists so serious buyers can learn about them and reach us directly.

That narrow focus matters in desert country. Water access, easements, soil, flood history, and county zoning each change what a parcel can become. Because we are the owners, we can speak to those details without filtering through a third-party agent.

If you are comparing raw land in West Texas, we welcome the conversation — but only for the two holdings we actually control. We are not sourcing other properties and we are not representing outside sellers.

What buyers can expect

  • Direct access to the owners on every inquiry
  • Clear disclosure on title, boundaries, and access for each parcel
  • Survey and documentation provided to qualified buyers
  • No pressure — desert land deserves a deliberate decision
  • Site visits coordinated when you are ready to see the ground
  • Straight answers on county requirements and practical limitations

The Process

How a purchase inquiry works.

1. Inquiry

Tell us which parcel interests you and what you intend to do with the land. Include your timeline and any questions about access, utilities, or build potential.

2. Documentation

We share parcel-specific details — acreage, survey references, title summary, and any known easements or restrictions — for qualified buyers.

3. Site & Terms

Arrange a visit to walk the ground, then discuss price and closing structure directly with the owners. No intermediary required.
